# TideLine

A cosy 3D fishing game on the Illawarra coast. Walk down to the water, cast a line, fight the fish, then decide: keep it or release it? A browser game for NSW Stage 5 **Aquatic Technology 7–10 — Aquatic harvesting** (recreational fishing).

**Spots (five sites):** Illawarra beach and rock platform (Woonona–Bellambi: lifeguard tower, Norfolk Island pines, banksia scrub, sandstone platform with rock pools) · Lake Illawarra (a cream sand bank with a grassy edge, a plank jetty out to the channel, seagrass patches, a mangrove edge on one side, a bait-shop hut, a moored tinnie and pelicans) · **Belmore Basin** (phase 5, a Wollongong fishing harbour: a timber wharf on pylons with lamps and bollards, three moored trawlers, the fishers' co-op, a stone breakwall out to a lighthouse, kelp and rubble under the wharf; it has a dusk and a dark-blue night, and the squid come up into the wharf lamps) · **Offshore reef** (phase 6, by boat from Belmore Basin: open blue water about 20 m deep with a reef below, a drifting boat with a fish finder) · **Freshwater river** (phase 6: casuarinas, a deep rocky pool, a weir, bass and pest carp). **Every site now has time of day** (sunset glow, dark-blue night).

Forked from the DeepSwell engine (vanilla ES modules, vendored Three.js r169). Look: `docs/ART-BIBLE.md`. Plan: `docs/specs/2026-11-01-tideline-design.md`.

## Run locally

No build step.

```bash
python3 devserver.py 8451
```

Open http://localhost:8451 (teacher links and QR codes: `/teacher.html`). Add `?play` to skip the title screen, `?eco=seagrass` to pick the estuary, `?eco=harbour` for Belmore Basin, `?eco=reef` for the offshore reef, `?eco=river` for the river (a deep link opens a site even before it is unlocked, and `?play` skips the reef's boat-trip scene; `&time=night` or `&time=dusk` starts after dark at any site; `&season=winter` or `&month=6` closes bass keeping on the river), `?quality=low` for the school-laptop tier, `&flow=low|normal|high` for the river, `?ceremony=awards|milestone` to preview the club ceremony.

## Controls
Third-person: a high, near top-down camera follows your angler (pitch about 60 degrees, 12–18 m back).
- **Walk:** WASD (relative to the camera), Shift run. Z / C (or hold the right mouse button and drag, or drag on touch) turns the camera; the mouse wheel (or a pinch) zooms. Wading stops at chest depth. Click an animal for its species card.
- **Tackle:** T opens the tackle box (rig and bait; a labelled diagram of each rig). The HUD chip shows the tide, time, season, swell and your tackle. Tackle that suits the fish bites sooner and gives a bigger reel zone.
- **Fish:** R rod out/away · the angler turns to face the pointer · hold Space to wind up (a ring on the water shows where the cast will land), release to cast · Space to strike when the float dips · then the **catch bar**: hold Space (or press and hold the bar on touch) to lift the green zone, let go to let it fall; keep the fish inside it to fill the meter. Floating litter earns steward points; reef fish bolt for the reef line (touch it and you are bust off).
- **Land a fish:** a species you have not identified yet opens the **dichotomous key**: turn the 3D fish (drag, wheel or pinch to zoom) and answer the two-choice couplets until the key names it. Then the **brag mat**: slide the marker to the tail tip (within 1 cm is Accurate). Then **keep or release**: the rules stay hidden until you commit, then you get the rule and the reason for it. A release is a quick hands-on step (wet your hands, hold to unhook, under 10 s out of the water). Kept fish go in the **esky** (illegal ones are marked).
- **On the boat (offshore reef):** the deck is your whole world and it drifts over the reef (you and your bait go with it). Read the fish finder panel (bottom left; top left on a phone); **B** or its ⚓ button puts the anchor down on sand (refused on reef) and lifts it; the **?** button explains how a fish finder works.
- **Head home:** H or the Head home button (or walk to the bait shop at the lake / the beach-access path at Bellambi) ends the outing. About 1 outing in 3 a Fisheries Officer checks your esky (`?officer=1` forces it, `?officer=0` never). Then the outing summary.
- **Illawarra Fish Guide:** J. Every species across both spots with per-spot counters, tiers (Seen, Caught, Identified, Mastered), Bay Health and your Steward rating. **Logbook:** L (every fish, CSV export). P pause. Touch devices get an on-screen joystick and buttons (📗 guide, 📒 logbook, 🏠 head home).
- **Your angler:** the **character creator** opens the first time you press Start fishing (and once on an older save), and any time from Settings -> **Edit my angler** or the title's **My angler** button. A live, turnable 3D preview on a sand-and-jetty plinth; options: name (16 letters, basic profanity filter), skin tone (8), hair style (6: short, long, ponytail, buzz, curly, bun) and colour (8), hat (none, bucket, cap, straw, beanie, wide-brim) and colour (8), sunnies (none, round, sporty), shirt style (plain, stripes, flowers, fishing shirt) and colour (8), shorts colour (8), rod colour (8), Randomise. Everything is a real Blender mesh or colour slot (`tools/blender/tideline.py`); the choice is saved in `progress.angler`, the name appears in dialogue ("Nice catch, Kai!") and as the first column of the logbook CSV. `?play` skips the creator (tests, teacher links); add `?creator` to see it. Console: `game.setAppearance({ skin, hairStyle, hatStyle, hat, shirt, pattern, shorts, sunnies, rod })`.
- **The coast's people (phase 3):** nine original characters, built from the same angler rig with a fixed look and their own props, live on the coast and keep a daily routine (dawn / day / dusk / night; the in-game clock runs 3 minutes a second, so a day takes 8). Walk up to one (a name tag appears) and press **E** to talk (a green **TALK** button on touch). Menu: Ask about… (rules, licence, the fee, swaps, safety), Any tips? and Heard any gossip? (both rotate with today's tide, time and season; **gossip names a species, where and when, and marks it Seen in your guide**, toast "Added to your guide: Seen"), and Jobs and trades. **Show me** trades are checked against your Fish Guide and logbook (Mastered yellowfin bream; three releases under 10 s out of the water; an accurate measure of a flathead in its slot; a protected species released; litter pulled from the water...). **Quiz** trades ask 1-3 questions from the worksheet quiz bank (all right = the reward; wrong = the answer is explained and you try again the next in-game day: after your next outing or at midnight). Rewards go to `progress.inventory`: rigs and baits (a locked rig in the tackle box says who to ask, e.g. "Ask Old Tam on the jetty"), a tag kit and release weight (used at the release step), lure colours, hats (wear them from the quest log) and Sea Country story cards (Fish Guide, **Sea Country** tab). You start with the paternoster, running sinker and unweighted rigs and prawn and beach worm bait; everything else is unlocked by a trade or bought at the tackle shop (phase 4, below). **Q** opens the **quest log** (requests with who, where, what and progress; ready to hand in; completed; your kit). People nearby react: "Hey, that one's undersize, mate" if you keep an illegal fish within 15 m, a cheer for a big legal catch, a greeting by your angler name. At Bellambi in a swell of 2.5 m or more (`?swell=3` to test) Sione the lifeguard warns you off the rock platform, and if you stay on it you get a safety mark against your Steward rating. Pelicans waddle round the fish-cleaning table at the lake, gulls gather over schools of fish near you and a tern dives onto them.
  - **Who:** Lake Illawarra: **Mitra** (bait-shop owner), **Old Tam** (jetty regular), **Dr Priya** (marine scientist, dinghy landing), **Ranger Jo** (Sea Country ranger, mangrove edge), **Mika** (kid angler, moves between spots) and the **Fisheries Officer** (patrols). Bellambi: **Sione** (lifeguard), **Bev** (fishing-club organiser, beach-path board), **Fatima** (clean-up volunteer), Mika and the officer. Dialogue, schedules, trades and quiz picks are data in `src/content/npcs.js`; rewards and Sea Country cards in `src/content/items.js`; the worksheet quiz bank in `src/content/quiz.js`.
  - **Needs review before classroom release:** the Sea Country ranger's dialogue and the six Sea Country story cards (`jo` in `src/content/npcs.js`, `CARDS` in `src/content/items.js`). They use only dataset section D rows tagged [worksheet] (nothing [unverified]), speak of the practices as living knowledge, say plainly that the ranger is not a Knowledge Holder and point students to one through the AECG, and summarise and link the D'harawal calendar (Bureau of Meteorology) instead of copying it. They must still be checked by the school's **Aboriginal Education Officer / local AECG** before the game is used with a class. Also check the link text (bom.gov.au, Indigenous Weather Knowledge, D'harawal calendar).
- **Economy and the tackle shop (phase 4).** NSW recreational fishers **cannot sell their catch**, so no fish has a price: **Club credits** (🪙, in the HUD next to Esky and Steward) are paid only for citizen science and good care, from `src/economy/coins.js` (`addCoins` refuses any other source; a unit test asserts there is no path from fish to credits). Numbers are in `RATES` in `src/content/shop.js`:
  - **Logbook record: 6** (species Identified + measure within 1 cm + the right keep-or-release call), **+15** first record of a species, **+4** a new spot/tide/time for that species; only **3 records of one species** pay per in-game day. A kept fish pays the same as a released one.
  - **Tag and release: 10** (Dr Priya's kit, legal-size fish, wet hands and 10 s or less; 5 a day). A **recapture letter** (40% per outing once a tagged fish is from an earlier day) pays **25** and explains what recaptures tell scientists.
  - **Club comp (Bev, Bellambi, "Today's club comp"):** one a day (most species released / longest legal measured dusky flathead / most accurate measures), free to enter, settled when you head home against three rivals: **40 / 25 / 15, then 5** for any score.
  - **Missions:** 6 per quiz question or 15 to 25 for a show-me job, on top of the item reward. **Litter:** **2** a piece on **Fatima's clean-up tally** (10 pieces a day).
  - Mitra's "Can I sell what I catch?" / "Sell my fish" lesson explains the law and lists these; the first time the esky has fish and you open the shop, a one-time tip says the same.
  - **Mitra's tackle shop** (talk to her, **Shop**; data in `src/content/shop.js`, rules in `src/economy/store.js`, UI in `src/ui/shop.js`): **rods** (4 tiers, 0 / 60 / 150 / 300; a wider catch-bar zone, a slower meter drain and slower fish; tiers 2 to 4 need 3 / 6 species Identified and 2 Mastered, so money alone does not skip learning), **reels and line** (standard, light 2 kg fluorocarbon 40: shy bream and luderick bite 30% more but bolting fish are harder to turn; strong 8 kg braid 90: a 50% chance to hold a bolting fish, but shy fish bite 20% less), **bait** (frozen prawns 8, pilchards 10, squid strips 12, ten a pack; bait is used one per bite), **gear that helps the fish** (release weight 30, knotless landing net 45, fine measuring mat 55 with half-centimetre marks) and **style** (hats, shirts and rod colours that use the creator's look data).
  - **Collected bait is free** but needs the right spot and item and respects the DPIRD daily limits: nippers (lake flats, nipper pump, 100 a day), beach worms (Bellambi beach, 20 a day), green weed (rocks, Old Tam's tip). Collect from the tackle box (T), which also shows what is left of each bait.
  - The gear stats (`gearStats(progress)`) are read by the catch bar (`barParams` in `src/fishing/catchbar.js`) and the bite model (`ctx.shy`, `ctx.lureMod` in `src/fishing/catches.js`); nothing is hard-coded in `fishing.js`.
  - The Phase 3 toys now do something: the **tag kit** is the tag-and-release step; the **release weight** lifts a deep-water fish's survival (snapper, kingfish, morwong lose about 30% to barotrauma without one), shown as an estimated survival on the release card with a small Bay Health bonus; **lure colours** give a bite modifier of about plus or minus 15% that varies with water clarity (from the swell) and time of day; a **landing net** keeps the fish in the water so the unhook clock runs a third slower.
  - Save schema is **v3** (`coins`, `econ`, `shop`); `migrateProgress` in `src/core/save.js` brings a v2 save forward, keeping all progress, with a 40-credit grant and the starting bait (a new save starts with 20).
- **Belmore Basin (phase 5).** The third site on the picker. It **opens once 5 species are Identified** in the Fish Guide (the card shows the requirement and your count, `unlock` in `src/ecosystems/harbour.js`, rule in `src/ecosystems/unlock.js`). Three spots: the **wharf** (pylons; lamps), the **breakwall** (rock; in a swell of 2.5 m or more it is washed by waves: spray, then a warning from Nell and a safety mark if you stay, `hazard` in the site) and the **basin** (calm, sandy). The harbour is the first site with **time of day** (`eco.daylight`, `src/ocean/daylight.js`, applied by `Environment.setDaylight`): warm sunset at dusk, then a dark-blue moonlit night with stars (never black), and the six wharf lamps come on. The lamps are shader pools of warm light on the water and the deck (`uLamps` in `ocean/shaders.js` and `ocean/water.js`); baitfish swirl in each pool and **squid and cuttlefish hover at the edge of the light** at dusk and night only (`src/world/harbourLife.js`).
  - **Squid jigging.** Squid and cuttlefish bite **only on a squid jig and only at dusk and night** (`gate` in `species.js`, `gateOpen` in `catches.js`), and a cast that lands in a lamp pool bites more (`ctx.lit`). The jig is for sale at Mitra's shop (**Lures**, 14 credits) or comes from her lake quiz. The catch bar has its own **jet profile**: a soft pull that creeps the bar down, a squeeze (warning), then a jet burst with a puff of ink (`PROFILES.jet` in `fishing/catchbar.js`), and a squid icon on a navy track. Landing a squid or cuttlefish splashes ink across the screen and the deck (`inkSplash` in `main.js`). **Bag limit: 20 squid and cuttlefish together** (DPIRD, dataset A2), counted as one bag (`rules.bagGroup`) and said so on the verdict card. New species from the dataset: **yellowtail scad (yakka)**, **slimy mackerel** and **cuttlefish** (squid is the southern calamari); the key grows to 22 species and 21 couplets, generated from `FEATURES`.
  - **People.** **Old Tam** now fishes the lake jetty by day and dusk and walks up to the harbour wharf at night. **Nell** (co-op fisher, original) is on the quay by day: what a co-op is, why recreational fishers cannot sell their catch while licensed commercial fishers can, nets, mesh size, licences, quotas and closures, bycatch (BRD and TED), and a **teaser** for the post-game commercial licence (nothing is unlocked). A show-me ("Name a squid at the wharf", checked against the Fish Guide, a `identified` need) and a quiz (nets, TED, quota: worksheet 2.2 questions). The co-op sale route is from the owner's spec; the dataset only says Illawarra boats work from Wollongong Harbour and sell via the Sydney Fish Market auction, so Nell's lines say no more than that.
  - **Release survival.** The release card shows the DPIRD **range** (bream 72 to 97%, dataset C13; snapper 67 to 92, mulloway 73 to 81, flathead 91 to 96) and says the estimate starts from its middle (`survivalNote` in `economy/effects.js`). The 12% to over 85% for a **deeply hooked bream whose line is cut** (C15) is a separate note on the bream's release card, not the survival figure.

- **Offshore reef, by boat (phase 6, site 4).** It **opens once you own the Estuary spin rod (or better) and have 8 species Identified** (the picker shows both parts, ticked or not: `unlock` `{ type: 'all', parts }` in `src/ecosystems/reef.js`, rule in `unlock.js`). **Start fishing** runs a **safety beat** first (`src/ui/boatTrip.js`): put your **lifejacket** on and check the fit (the dataset's "Boat: everyone wears a PFD", C28, and C26's lift test), **log your trip** (who is aboard, back in 2 or 4 hours, a general safety tip with no legal claims) and read the swell (the BOM Coasts and oceans page, E1), then a short painted **boat-trip scene** out past the Belmore Basin lighthouse (the Five Islands rise on the horizon; Skip works). The outing summary shows the trip log. The boat is the walkable land: a 7.4 m centre-console whose deck is a small moving rectangle (`src/world/boatModels.js`, `deckAt` in `reef.js`); it **drifts** across the reef (about 0.6 m a second, `driftSpeed`, a motor-back-upwind blink at the edge of the area) and carries you and your bait with it. **B** (or the panel button) puts the **anchor** down, **never on the reef**: over hard bottom or rubble it is refused with the reason (an anchor would snag and tear the reef up; this is gameplay, not a claim from the dataset), over sand it holds.
  - **Fish finder** (`src/fishing/sonar.js` pure and deterministic given the seed; `src/ui/sonarUI.js` draws it): a scrolling screen (newest ping on the right) with the depth, a **bottom line** (thick and bright with a second echo over hard reef, thin over sand), **arches** (single fish: the 60-degree cone catches a fish first at an angle, then straight below, then at an angle again) and a **cloud of dots** (baitfish). A mark straight under the boat marks that fish Seen in the Fish Guide, and a mark under your bait raises that species' bite chance (`ctx.arch`). The **(?) card** teaches how it works (sound pulses echo off the bottom and swim bladders: dataset E1, worksheets 2.1.7 and 2.1.13) and that a fish finder is a **harvesting technology** (recreational and commercial; it can make over-fishing easier, so bag and size limits apply regardless of gear). The swim-bladder-gas explanation is general sonar science (tagged [unverified] in `sonar.js`); no rule depends on it.
  - **Spots and species:** the reef (hard bottom), the reef edge and the sand beside it (chosen from the bottom where the float lands). Snapper (reused; bag 10, min 30), kingfish (65, 5), red morwong (30, 5), **jackass morwong (new: 30 cm, bag 10, possession 10, DEPLETED Eastern stock, swap to snapper or kingfish, dataset A2 and B1)**, silver trevally, bonito, Australian salmon, tailor, blue groper and wobbegong (protected, release only). A leatherjacket is not in the dataset, so it is not in the game.
  - **Barotrauma:** the release weight and the survival card shine here. A deep-water fish (snapper, kingfish, red and jackass morwong) caught at **10 m or more** (C17; the reef catch records its depth from `eco.depthAt`) loses about 30% without the weight and the card says why; caught shallower (the top of a bommie) the card says barotrauma is not a concern there. Survival ranges are dataset C13 only (snapper 67 to 92%, kingfish 85%); the morwongs have no published figure, so their card says so and starts from a standard 90% estimate.
- **Freshwater river (phase 6, site 5).** An Illawarra / Shoalhaven-style coastal river (`src/ecosystems/river.js`): casuarina (river she-oak) banks, a deep still pool among boulders, a concrete **weir** you can walk across, fallen snags and a licence noticeboard. Opens at **6 species Identified**. Spots: the rocky pool, below the weir and the snags. Species: **Australian bass** (no minimum; bag 2 with estuary perch, possession 4, **only 1 over 35 cm**, and a **closed season May to August in rivers**: `?season=winter` or `?month=5..8` makes every bass catch and release only, the HUD chip says so, the verdict says why: so the fish can spawn; dataset A2 and A3), **common carp** (an introduced pest) and the shortfin eel (the dataset has no other freshwater fish this game uses; estuary perch and redfin are not included).
  - **The carp flow teaches BEST PRACTICE, never law** (owner's spec; dataset B2): keeping a carp takes it out of the river (it is not an esky fish, it counts as a pest taken out, and it passes Mastery); returning one is allowed but the card says it is "not best practice", that DPIRD encourages keeping and humanely disposing of pest fish, that it is not a size or bag rule and that other states differ; a small Steward cost. No card says illegal, must or offence (`src/fishing/pest.js`, tested). **Needs checking before classroom release:** the carp best-practice wording is tagged [DPIRD-search] in the dataset (seen only in a search summary), so confirm it on the DPIRD pest-fish page.
  - **Licence note** (shown once, and painted on the noticeboard): the recreational fishing fee (3 days $7, 1 month $14, 1 year $35, 3 years $85, "check the date"), under 18 pays nothing but follows every rule, the money goes to the Recreational Fishing Trusts (saltwater and freshwater), check FishSmart: all dataset C9 to C12, in `LICENCE` in `src/content/river.js`.
- **Time of day at every site (phase 6).** The harbour's day, dusk and night lighting is now one shared system (`src/ocean/daylight.js`: `lightingFor(eco)`, `paletteAt(water, hour, cfg)`; every site sets `daylight: true`, and can tune `{ glow, night, lamps }`), applied by `Environment.setDaylight`. **Dusk has a stronger, warmer sunset**: a long glow (peak about 18:40), an amber-orange horizon and a rose-violet sky, an orange low sun, peachy shallows and a warm multiply wash over the whole picture (`#fx-sunset`, every graphics tier); the night is still dark blue and readable, never black. A new day starts just after sunrise (07:36) so the first outing is bright, and the 8-minute clock runs on through dusk and night. Only the harbour has lamps.
- **Phase 5 polish.** The squid's eyes are now flush, set into the head like a lens in a socket (not bug-eyes), and squid and cuttlefish glow in the lamp light (a chromatophore pulse in the skin plus a pink halo that reads from the top-down camera). The **harbour quay** is a place: fish-cleaning tables with buckets and eskies, crates and nets, a diesel bowser in a yellow chevron bay, a café kiosk with striped awning and four umbrella tables on a paved plaza, planters, net-drying racks, crab pots, wheelie bins, boat trailers in painted parking bays (the paint is a canvas decal, `src/world/quayPaint.js`, because vertex colours are far too coarse for paint lines). The **trawlers** are solid lofted hulls (rounded bilge, sheer stripe, red antifouling, a transom) with a deck, bulwarks, a wheelhouse with big windows, a mast and radar dome, **folded outriggers**, an A-frame with trawl doors, a net drum and net, and strings of floats. The **breakwall boulders** are angular, flat-faced grey-brown armour rock with only a trace of moss.
- **Mastery:** Identified + an accurate measure + a **legal keep, or a correct release of a fish you could not keep** (undersize, over the slot, over the bag, protected). Releasing a legal fish is always allowed but is neutral: it does not count toward Mastery.

## Phase 7: the ending, the co-op licence, the teacher page, ship polish

- **Endings** (`src/fishing/ending.js`, `src/content/ceremony.js`, `src/ui/ceremony.js`). Pure triggers: **100% Caught** (every one of the 25 species) gives a smaller club celebration; **100% Mastered and Bay Health at 73% or more** (`BAY_THRESHOLD`; Bay Health = the native fish stocks, carp excluded, plus a Steward bonus of up to +5.4 for well-handled releases, tags, litter and pest fish removed, and a penalty for illegal keeps: `fishing/guide.js` `stewardBonus`; shown to one decimal, rounded down) is **awards night**, the ending. Each is shown once (`progress.ending`), offered after heading home ("Go to the club" on the outing summary), by the title screen's Awards night button, or from Bev. The ceremony is an evening on Bellambi beach: bunting, lanterns, the whole cast of ten in a half circle, and each person gives an award that quotes the player's own numbers (releases, accurate measures, litter, tags, Bay Health, Steward stars, safety marks). Then the end card with the tagline "Gotta catch 'em all... and release the small.", the credits and **Keep fishing** (the next morning on the beach; the game carries on). `?ceremony=awards` or `?ceremony=milestone` previews it and saves nothing (not the ceremony, not the day). Awards night also marks the milestone as seen.
- **Co-op licence** (`src/content/coop.js`, `src/economy/commercial.js`, `src/ui/coop.js`). Nell (Belmore Basin) offers it only after a real ceremony has been held (the 100% Caught celebration or awards night; being 100% Identified does not open it). It is the commercial chapter in four tabs: a **Nets lab** (gear: handline, trap, longline, gill net, purse seine, trawl; a stretched-mesh slider; a BRD and a TED; a deterministic haul simulation with target, bycatch, young fish, protected animals and seabed disturbance; lab goals), **Sell at the co-op** (land a commercial haul with your method, a quota per species and per quota year, three hauls a day, sell for **co-op credits**), **Sustainability** (the six SAFS statuses, the 316/503 gauge, quotas and prices by status, the dataset B1 swaps, a pointer to GoodFish) and **Boat** (sonar, GPS, refrigeration, autopilot). A depleted stock (jackass morwong) is closed; a recovering one (mulloway) has a small quota and a lower price. **Selling:** `sellHold` is the only place a fish becomes money, it needs the licence, and it takes only a **commercial** lot; the recreational esky is never an input (`refuseEskySale` always refuses, and the panel has a button that shows why). Co-op credits (`progress.coop.credits`) are a separate currency from Club credits and `economy/coins.js`/`store.js` know nothing about the co-op (unit-tested, including a static scan). The worksheets give levels (Low/High), not numbers, so the haul percentages, prices and quotas are game numbers and the screen says so. ITQs and GoodFish colour ratings are not in the dataset and are not taught.
- **The river has flow, not tide** (`DayConditions.setFresh`, `eco.fresh`). The HUD says "Normal flow", "After rain: high flow" or "Low flow" (`?flow=low|normal|high`), the bite model gets `tide: null` and a `flow`, high flow gives bass and eels a better bite and murkier water for the lure colours, and the logbook column reads "tide or river flow".
- **River look**: the default spawn is on a raised bank pad with a timber landing; a stepped concrete weir with a fish ladder ("FISHWAY" sign only; the dataset has no fish passage facts, so there is no teaching card), white water and foam; bark-textured snags with root balls; she-oak, water gum and lomandra, reeds, pebble bars and rocks (`src/world/riverTrees.js`).
- **Save v4** adds `ending` and `coop` (additive; v1 to v3 saves migrate and nothing is lost).
- **Teacher page** (`teacher.html`): at-a-glance, a 4-lesson sequence, the deep-link table and a link/QR builder, the logbook export, a syllabus map to the Module 2 worksheets, and the data sources and caveats box. It prints cleanly.
- **Ship polish**: title screen with Continue / New game (asks first), Settings, Teacher page, Credits and a small "Learning Hub" link; a loading bar that fills as the scripts arrive, a "slow connection" note and a failure message with Try again; Open Graph and description tags, an SVG favicon and an apple touch icon; `.nojekyll`; every path is relative so it works under `/tideline/`.
- **Credits** say "Sea Country content pending AEO/AECG review" and so does the teacher page: the Sea Country ranger, her cards and the cultural quiz must be checked by the school AEO / local AECG before a class uses them.

## Release fixes (pre-release pass)

- **One simulated day** (`src/economy/possession.js`): the day (`progress.npc.day`) moves on when you head home after fishing, and at midnight. Bag limits, "one bass over 35 cm", bait collecting limits, credit caps and failed quizzes reset together; nothing uses the computer's date. Heading home with nothing done does not start a new day. Fish you take home go in the **fridge** (`progress.fridge`) and count toward possession (dataset C6) for three simulated days; fish an officer takes never count.
- **Stocks** recover toward their seed by 2 points a day, and a species at zero stock keeps a small bite weight (`STOCK_FLOOR`), so it can always be found and Mastered.
- **Saves** are repaired field by field (`migrateProgress`, listed in `progress.repaired`); a save that cannot be read is first copied to `tideline.progress.v1.backup.<time>`.
- The people (the angler, Mitra, Nell, everyone) load in every graphics style, and are built from simple shapes if the model file cannot be fetched.
